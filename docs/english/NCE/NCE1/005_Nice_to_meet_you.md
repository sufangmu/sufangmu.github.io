## Lesson 5 Nice to meet you.

=== "原文"
    Good morning.

    Good morning Mr. Blake.

    This is Miss Sophie Dupont.

    Sophie is a new student.

    She is French.

    Sophie this is Hans.

    He is German.

    Nice to meet you.

    And this is Naoko.

    She's Japanese.

    Nice to meet you.

    And this is Chang-woo.

    He's Korean.

    Nice to meet you.

    And this is Luming.

    He's Chinese.

    Nice to meet you.

    And this is Xiaohui.

    She is Chinese too.

    Nice to meet you.
