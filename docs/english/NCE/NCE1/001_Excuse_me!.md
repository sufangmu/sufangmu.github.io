## Lesson 1 Excuse me!

Excuse me!

Yes?

Is this your handbag?

Pardon?

Is this your handbag?

Yes it is.

Thank you very much.

### Expressions

- Excuse me! 打扰一下；借过
- Yes? 什么事？
- Is this your...? 这是你的……吗？
- Pardon? 请再说一遍
- Thank you very much 非常感谢
