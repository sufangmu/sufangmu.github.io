## Lesson 27 Mrs. Smith's living room

=== "原文"
    Mrs. Smith's living room is large.

    There is a television in the room.

    The television is near the window.

    There are some magazines on the television.

    There is a table in the room.

    There are some newspapers on the table.

    There are some armchairs in the room.

    The armchairs are near the table.

    There is a stereo in the room.

    The stereo is near the door.

    There are some books on the stereo.

    There are some pictures in the room.

    The pictures are on the wall.

=== "译文"
    史密斯太太的客厅很大。

    客厅里有台电视机。

    电视机靠近窗户。

    电视机上有几本杂志。

    客厅里有张桌子。

    桌子上有几份报纸。

    客厅里有几把扶手椅。

    扶手椅靠近桌子。

    客厅里有台立体声音响。

    音响靠近门。

    音响上有几本书。

    客厅里有几幅画。

    画挂在墙上。

### Words

- living room /ˈlɪvɪŋ rum/ 客厅
    1. This is my living room.
    2. There is a table in the living room.
    3. Is there a table in the living room?
- near /nɪr/ *prep.* 在……附近
    1. The man is near the woman.
    2. Is the man near the woman?
    3. Where is the man?
- window /ˈwɪndo/ *n.* 窗户
    1. The woman is near the window.
    2. Is the woman near the window?
    3. Where is the woman?
- armchair /ˈɑrmˌtʃɛr/ 扶手椅
    1. There are two armchairs in the living room.
    2. There are three armchairs in the living room.
    3. Are there three armchairs in the living room?
    4. How many armchairs are there in the living room?
- door /dɔr/ 门
    1. The boy is near the door.
    2. Is the boy near the door?
    3. Where is the boy?
- picture /ˈpɪktʃɚ/ 图画
    1. There are some pictures near the TV.
- wall /wɔl/ 墙
    1. The pictures are on the wall.
    2. There are some pictures on the wall.
    3. There are three cats in the picture.
    4. Are there three cats in the picture?
    5. How many cats are there in the picture?
    6. There are four windows in my living room.
    7. Are there four windows in your living room?
    8. How many windows are there in your living room?
    9. There are…
    10. There are some chairs in the middle of the room.
- trousers /ˈtraʊzɚz/ 裤子
