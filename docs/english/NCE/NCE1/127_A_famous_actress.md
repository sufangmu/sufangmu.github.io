## Lesson 127 A famous actress

=== "原文"
    Can you recognize that woman, Liz?

    I think I can, Kate. It must be Karen Marsh, the actress.

    I thought so. Who's that beside her?

    That must be Conrad Reeves.

    Conrad Reeves, the actor? It can't be. Let me have another look. I think you're right! Isn't he her third husband?

    No. He must be her fourth or fifth.

    Doesn't Karen Marsh look old!

    She does, doesn't she! I read she's twenty-nine, but she must be at least forty.

    I'm sure she is.

    She was a famous actress when I was still at school.

    That was a long time ago, wasn't it?

    Not that long ago! I'm not more than twenty-nine myself.

=== "译文"
    你能认出那个女人吗，莉兹？

    我想我能，凯特。那一定是卡伦·马什，那位女演员。

    我也这么想。她旁边的是谁？

    那一定是康拉德·里夫斯。

    康拉德·里夫斯，那个男演员？不可能。让我再看一眼。我想你是对的！他不是她的第三任丈夫吗？

    不。他一定是她的第四任或第五任丈夫。

    卡伦·马什看上去真老！

    是的，不是吗！我读到她29岁，但她一定至少40岁了。

    我肯定她至少40岁。

    当我还在上学时，她就是一个著名的女演员了。

    那是很久以前的事了，不是吗？

    也没那么久！我自己还不到29岁呢。

### Words

- famous /ˈfeməs/ *n.* 著名的
    1. She is a famous actress.
    2. The famous actress who you saw last night is my sister.
    3. When the famous actress was having a bath, someone knocked at the door.
- actress /ˈæktrəs/ 女演员
- read /rid/ 读
    1. Can’t you read the sign?
    2. It says “No Parking”.
    3. I read the actress was here last night.（L99）
    4. Marsh is?
    5. She thinks she must be at least forty.
    6. Why does Liz say Karen Marsh must be at least forty?
    7. Because Karen Marsh was a famous actress when Liz was still at school.
    8. Does Kate think that was a long time ago?
    9. Yes, she does.
    10. How old is Liz now?
- actor /ˈæktɚ/ *n.* 男演员
    1. The actor who you met yesterday is my brother.
    2. This is the best actor I’ve ever seen.（L111）
    3. The actor had to get up very early this morning.
    4. Kate is with Liz, isn't she?
    5. Yes, she is.
    6. Can Liz recognize Karen Marsh?
    7. Yes, she can.
    8. Who is Karen Marsh?
    9. She is a famous actress.
    10. Marsh?
