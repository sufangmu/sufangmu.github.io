## Lesson 59 Is that all?

=== "原文"
    I want some envelopes, please.

    Do you want the large size or the small size?

    The large size, please.

    Do you have any writing paper?

    Yes, we do.

    I don't have any small pads. I only have large ones. Do you want a pad?

    Yes, please.

    And I want some glue.

    A bottle of glue.

    And I want a large box of chalk, too.

    I only have small boxes. Do you want one?

    No, thank you.

    Is that all?

    That's all, thank you.

    What else do you want?

    I want my change.

=== "译文"
    请给我一些信封。

    您要大号的还是小号的？

    请给我大号的。

    您有信纸吗？

    有的。

    我没有小号便签本。我只有大号的。您要一本便签本吗？

    好的，请来一本。

    我还要一些胶水。

    一瓶胶水。

    我还要一大盒粉笔。

    我只有小盒的。您要一盒吗？

    不，谢谢。

    就这些吗？

    就这些，谢谢。

    您还要什么？

    我要找的零钱。

### Words

- envelope /ˈɛnvəˌlop/ *n.* 信封
- writing paper /ˈraɪtɪŋ ˌpepɚ/ 信纸
    1. Don’t write on that writing paper.
    2. She is writing on that writing paper.
- size /saɪz/ 尺寸
    1. I want a large size.
    2. Do you want a large size?
    3. What size do you want?
    4. Where is the lady?
    5. She's at the stationer's.
    6. What does the lady want?
    7. What size envelopes does she want?
    8. The large size.
    9. What else does she want?
    10. She wants some writing paper.
- pad /pæd/ 便签本
    1. He wants a pad.
    2. Does he want a pad?
    3. What does he want?
    4. She is going to buy some pads.
- glue /ɡlu/ 胶水
    1. There is some glue in the kitchen.
    2. Is there any glue in the kitchen?
- chalk /tʃɔk/ 粉笔
    1. Look, the little girl is eating the chalk, stop that.
    2. Can you give me a box of chalk?
- change /tʃendʒ/ 零钱
    1. She wants her change.
    2. Does she want her change?
    3. Does the lady want a large pad?
    4. Yes, she does.
    5. Does she want some glue too?
    6. Does she want a box of chalk?
    7. Does she want the small size?
    8. No, she doesn't.
    9. What else does she want?
    10. Ask me if the lady wants any envelopes.
