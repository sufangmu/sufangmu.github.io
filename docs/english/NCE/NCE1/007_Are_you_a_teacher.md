## Lesson 7 Are you a teacher?

I am a new student. My name's Robert.

Nice to meet you. My name's Sophie.

Are you French?

Yes I am.
Are you French too?

No I am not.

What nationality are you?

I'm Italian.

Are you a teacher?

No I'm not.

What's your job?

I'm a keyboard operator.

What's your job?

I'm an engineer.
