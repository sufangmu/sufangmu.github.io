##  Lesson 3 Sorry sir.

My coat and my umbrella please.

Here is my ticket.

Thank you sir.

Number five.

Here's your umbrella and your coat.

This is not my umbrella.

Sorry sir.

Is this your umbrella?

No it isn't.

Is this it?

Yes it is.

Thank you very much.
