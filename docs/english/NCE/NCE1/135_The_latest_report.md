## Lesson 135 The latest report

=== "原文"
    Are you really going to retire, Miss Marsh?

    I may. I can't make up my mind. I will have to ask my future husband.
    He won't let me make another film.

    Your future husband, Miss Marsh?

    Yes. Let me introduce him to you. His name is Carlos. We're going to get married next week.

    Look, Liz! Here's another report about Karen Marsh. Listen: 'Karen Marsh: The latest. At her London Hotel today Miss Marsh told reporters she might retire. She said she couldn't make up her mind. She said she would have to ask her future husband. She said her future husband would not let her make another film. Then she introduced us to Carlos and told us they would get married next week.'

    That's sensational news, isn't it, Kate?

    It certainly is.He'll be her sixth husband!

=== "译文"
    您真的要退休吗，马什小姐？

    可能吧。我还下不了决心。我得问问我的未婚夫。
    他不会让我再拍电影的。

    您的未婚夫，马什小姐？

    是的。让我把他介绍给您。他叫卡洛斯。我们下周就要结婚了。

    看，莉兹！这里还有一篇关于卡伦·马什的报道。听："卡伦·马什：最新消息。今天在伦敦旅馆，马什小姐告诉记者她可能退休。她说她还下不了决心。她说她得问问她的未婚夫。她说她的未婚夫不会让她再拍电影。然后她把卡洛斯介绍给我们，告诉我们他们下周就要结婚了。"

    这真是轰动的消息，不是吗，凯特？

    当然是。他将是她的第六任丈夫！

### Words

- future /ˈfjutʃɚ/ *adj.* 未来的
    1. This is my future husband.
    2. While my future wife was having a bath, someone knocked on the door.（L117）
    3. My future husband is taller than your future wife.（L107）
- latest /ˈletɪst/ *adj.* 最新的
    1. This is the latest/newest model.
    2. The model which you saw yesterday is the latest one.
- introduce /ˌɪntrəˈdus/ *v.* 介绍
    1. I would like to introduce my boss.（L107）
    2. I had to introduce it to my boss yesterday.
    3. Did you have to introduce it to your boss yesterday?
    4. Does Karen Marsh say she's going to retire?
    5. She says she might.
    6. Who does she say she will have to ask?
    7. She says she will have to ask her future husband.
    8. When does she say she's going to get married?
    9. She says she's going to get married next week.
    10. Did Karen Marsh tell reporters she might retire?
- hotel /hoˈtɛl/ 旅馆
    1. This is the hotel which I told you about.（L121）
    2. This must be a very nice hotel.（L127）
    3. She can’t have been living in that hotel.（L129）
    4. They may be sleeping in that hotel.（L131）
