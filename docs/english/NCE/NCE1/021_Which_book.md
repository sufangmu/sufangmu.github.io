## Lesson 21 Which book?

=== "原文"
    Give me a book please, Jane.

    Which book?

    This one?

    No, not that one. The red one.

    This one?

    Yes, please.

    Here you are.

    Thank you.

=== "译文"
    请给我一本书，简。

    哪一本？

    这本？

    不，不是那本。那本红色的。

    这本？

    是的，请给我。

    给你。

    谢谢。
