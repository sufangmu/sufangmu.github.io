## Lesson 15 Your passports please.

Are you Swedish?

No we are not. We are Danish.

Are your friends Danish too?

No they aren't. They are Norwegian.

Your passports please.

Here they are.

Are these your cases?

No they aren't.

Our cases are brown.

Here they are.

Are you tourists?

Yes we are.

Are your friends tourists too?

Yes they are.

That's fine.

Thank you very much.
