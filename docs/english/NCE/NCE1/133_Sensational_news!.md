## Lesson 133 Sensational news!

=== "原文"
    Have you just made a new film, Miss Marsh?

    Yes, I have.

    Are you going to make another?

    No, I'm not. I'm going to retire. I feel very tired. I don't want to make another film for a long time.

    Let's buy a newspaper, Liz. Listen to this! 'Karen Marsh: Sensational News! By our reporter, Alan Jones. Karen Marsh arrived at London Airport today. She was wearing a blue dress and a mink coat. She told me she had just made a new film. she said she was not going to make another. She said she was going to retire. She told reporters she felt very tired and didn't want to make another film for a long time.'

    I wonder why!

=== "译文"
    您刚拍完一部新电影吗，马什小姐？

    是的，拍完了。

    您打算再拍一部吗？

    不，不打算了。我准备退休了。我觉得很累。我很长一段时间都不想再拍电影了。

    我们买份报纸吧，莉兹。听这个！"卡伦·马什：轰动的消息！由本报记者艾伦·琼斯报道。卡伦·马什今天到达伦敦机场。她穿着一件蓝色的连衣裙和一件貂皮大衣。她告诉我她刚拍完一部新电影。她说她不打算再拍了。她说她准备退休了。她告诉记者们她觉得很累，很长一段时间都不想再拍电影了。"

    我想知道为什么！
