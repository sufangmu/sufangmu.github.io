## Lesson 107 It's too small.

=== "原文"
    Do you like this dress, madam?

    I like the colour very much. It's a lovely dress, but it's too small for me.

    What about this one? It's a lovely dress. It's very smart. Short skirts are in fashion now. Would you like to try it?

    All right.

    I'm afraid this green dress is too small for me as well. It's smaller than the blue one.

    I don't like the colour either. It doesn't suit me at all. I think the blue dress is prettier.

    Could you show me another blue dress? I want a dress like that one, but it must be my size.

    I'm afraid I haven't got a larger dress. This is the largest dress in the shop.

=== "译文"
    您喜欢这件连衣裙吗，夫人？

    我很喜欢这颜色。这是一件漂亮的连衣裙，但对我来说太小了。

    这件怎么样？这是一件漂亮的连衣裙。很时髦。短裙现在很流行。您想试试吗？

    好吧。

    恐怕这件绿色的连衣裙对我来说也太小了。它比蓝色的那件还小。

    我也不喜欢这颜色。它根本不适合我。我觉得蓝色的那件更漂亮。

    您能给我看看另一件蓝色的连衣裙吗？我想要一件像那样的，但必须是我的尺码。

    恐怕我没有更大的了。这是店里最大的一件连衣裙了。
